My story

Orbit calculations

One of my favourite classes in high school had us calculate the energy needed to get into Orbit. This class made me realise how much i loved science and knowing how the basics work. The fact there are now calculators that do it for you is kind of sad. This memory really stays with me asit was one of the few classes i attended and enjoyed.

Coffee and Pastries

One of my biggest personal interests has become coffee and pastries. When i moved to Sweden i started to understand the Fika culture. After moving back to the Netherlands i really missed this and as such decided to host a cafe at home. To this day it is one of my proudest achievements, I hosted for around 30 people and felt so loved being surrounded by my friends.

Colliding Hobbies

This is one of the fun things I have done and enjoy daily. I modded my cofee machine, combining cofee and programming. With this the machine now is controlled by a little microcontroller and can be programmed to extract the coffee in different ways.
